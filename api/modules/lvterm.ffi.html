Module lvterm.ffi

LuaJIT FFI backend: libvterm called directly, with nothing to compile.

Only what lvterm needs of libvterm 0.3 is declared: feed it bytes, then look at the cells and the cursor, and ask it what a key or the mouse sends. Four of its callbacks are used, to keep the lines that scroll off the top, to know when the program switches screens, hides the cursor, asks for the mouse or sets the title, to count the bells, and to take what the terminal sends back, so that a handle has that to give out in order.
